Third-party material
====================

Moby seccomp profile
--------------------

docker/seccomp/moby-default-seccomp.json is based on the Moby Profiles
seccomp/default.json at https://github.com/moby/profiles/tree/seccomp/v0.2.3.
The upstream project licenses this material under Apache-2.0. Its root at that
tag lists LICENSE and no NOTICE file. The complete license text is in
LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt.

Provider signing keys
---------------------

docker/1password.asc is the 1Password public key sourced from
https://downloads.1password.com/linux/keys/1password.asc.

docker/cloud.google.gpg is the Google Cloud package signing key produced from
https://packages.cloud.google.com/apt/doc/apt-key.gpg.

The licensing terms for these public-key files have not been established from
an authoritative source. They are intentionally excluded from REUSE license
coverage and no license expression is asserted for them. Their bytes are kept
unchanged.
